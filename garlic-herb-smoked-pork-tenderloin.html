Garlic-Herb Smoked Pork Tenderloin with Wild Rice Mushroom Pilaf

Thursday dinner. Lean smoked pork tenderloin with wild rice and mushrooms, roasted carrots and broccoli.

Yield: 6 servings · Prep: 25 minutes · Cook: 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 lb Pork tenderloin; raw, approximately two tenderloins; smoke Saturday and freeze for Thursday
  • 1.5 cup Wild rice blend; dry
  • 12 oz Mushrooms; sliced
  • 1.5 lb Carrots; sliced
  • 1.5 lb Broccoli florets
  • 3 cup Low-sodium chicken broth; for rice, or use water per package
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil; divided
  • 5 clove Garlic; minced
  • 2 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 2 tsp Dried thyme
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1.5 tsp Kosher salt; divided
  • 0.75 tsp Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Rub pork with Dijon, 1 tbsp olive oil, rosemary, thyme, half the garlic, salt and pepper.
  2. On Saturday smoke at 250–275°F until the thickest portion reaches 145°F. Rest at least 3 minutes before slicing. Chill promptly, freeze for Thursday, and thaw in refrigerator beginning Wednesday.
  3. Cook wild rice blend in broth according to package directions.
  4. Sauté mushrooms with 1 tbsp olive oil and remaining garlic until browned. Stir into cooked rice.
  5. Toss carrots and broccoli with remaining olive oil, salt and pepper. Roast at 425°F for 20–30 minutes until tender.
  6. Gently reheat sliced pork with a splash of broth until steaming hot (165°F for leftovers), avoiding drying it out.
  7. Divide pork, rice pilaf and vegetables into six portions; refrigerate two for Friday lunch.
