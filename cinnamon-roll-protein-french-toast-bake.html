Cinnamon Roll Protein French Toast Bake

Sunday family breakfast. Make-ahead cinnamon French toast casserole with light cream-cheese yogurt drizzle; easy to reheat for late risers.

Yield: 4 servings · Prep: 20 minutes · Cook: 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 12 slice Whole-grain bread; about 14–16 oz, cubed
  • 4 whole Large eggs
  • 1 cup Liquid egg whites
  • 1.5 cup Milk, 2%
  • 2 scoop Vanilla protein powder; approximately 60 g
  • 2 tsp Ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp Vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp Maple syrup
  • 4 oz Light cream cheese; softened
  • 0.75 cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp Powdered sugar; optional for glaze
  • 0.25 cup Chopped pecans; optional topping
  • 1 portion Cooking spray; for baking dish

Instructions

  1. Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish. Add cubed bread in an even layer.
  2. Whisk eggs, egg whites, milk, protein powder, cinnamon, vanilla and maple syrup until smooth. Pour over bread and press gently to moisten.
  3. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 30 minutes).
  4. Bake uncovered at 350°F for 40–45 minutes until center is set and top is lightly browned. If browning too quickly, tent loosely with foil.
  5. Whisk softened cream cheese with Greek yogurt and optional powdered sugar until smooth. Thin with a little milk if needed.
  6. Divide casserole into four portions and drizzle with glaze. Add optional pecans.
  7. Cool and refrigerate leftovers promptly; reheat individual portions until hot.
