Maple-Dijon Smoked Chicken Thighs with Sweet Potatoes and Green Beans

Tuesday dinner. Sweet-savory smoked thighs, roasted sweet potato wedges and garlic green beans.

Yield: 6 servings · Prep: 20 minutes · Cook: 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2.75 lb Boneless skinless chicken thighs; raw; smoke Saturday and freeze for Tuesday
  • 3 lb Sweet potatoes; cut into wedges
  • 1.75 lb Green beans; trimmed
  • 3 tbsp Pure maple syrup
  • 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil; divided
  • 4 clove Garlic; minced
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1.5 tsp Kosher salt; divided
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Whisk maple syrup, Dijon and vinegar. Reserve half in a clean container for serving; do not reuse sauce that has touched raw chicken.
  2. Season thighs with smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Smoke Saturday at 275°F until 175–185°F internal for tender thighs; brush with some maple-Dijon glaze toward the end.
  3. Cool promptly and freeze Tuesday portions after chilling. Thaw in refrigerator beginning Monday.
  4. On Tuesday heat oven to 425°F. Toss sweet potato wedges with 1 tbsp olive oil and seasoning; roast 30–40 minutes, flipping halfway.
  5. Toss green beans with remaining olive oil and garlic. Roast 15–20 minutes on a second pan.
  6. Reheat chicken covered with a splash of water or reserved juices until 165°F. Serve with vegetables and clean reserved maple-Dijon sauce.
  7. Portion six servings and refrigerate two promptly for Wednesday lunch.
