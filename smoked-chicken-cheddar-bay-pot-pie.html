Smoked Chicken Cheddar Bay Biscuit Pot Pie

Creamy smoked chicken and vegetable filling topped with cheddar-garlic drop biscuits.

6 servings · 20 min prep · 35 min cook

Ingredients

  • 600 g cooked skinless smoked chicken breast, diced or shredded
  • 400 g frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 234 g all-purpose flour (24 g filling; 210 g biscuits)
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cups 1% milk (1 1/4 cups filling; 3/4 cup biscuits)
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 1/4 tsp garlic powder (1 tsp biscuits; 1/4 tsp topping)
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter (2 tbsp cold for biscuits; 1 tbsp melted for topping)
  • 4 oz sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste for the filling
  • Black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Use a deep 9-by-13-inch baking dish or a large oven-safe skillet.
  2. Heat the olive oil over medium heat. Cook the onion for 4–5 minutes, until softened. Stir in 24 g flour and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Gradually whisk in the broth, then 1 1/4 cups milk. Add thyme and pepper. Simmer, stirring, until the sauce coats a spoon, about 4–6 minutes.
  4. Stir in the frozen vegetables and cooked smoked chicken. Simmer until hot and bubbling. Adjust the seasoning and transfer to the baking dish if needed.
  5. Whisk 210 g flour, baking powder, 1 tsp garlic powder and 1/2 tsp salt. Rub in 2 tbsp cold butter until crumbly. Stir in cheddar, then mix in 3/4 cup milk just until a sticky dough forms.
  6. Drop six mounds of dough over the hot filling, leaving gaps for steam. Bake for 18–23 minutes, until golden and cooked through in the biscuit centers.
  7. Mix 1 tbsp melted butter, 1/4 tsp garlic powder and parsley. Brush over the hot biscuits. Rest 5 minutes, then divide into six portions.

Estimated nutrition per serving

551 kcal · 45 g protein · 46 g carbs · 19.5 g fat. Excludes any oil, sugar, or glaze added when smoking the chicken. Weigh chicken after cooking.
