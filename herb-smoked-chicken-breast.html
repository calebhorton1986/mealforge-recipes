Herb-Smoked Chicken Breast with Garlic Potatoes & Brussels Sprouts

Herb-smoked chicken breast with crispy garlic potatoes, roasted Brussels sprouts and lemon-herb yogurt sauce.

Yield: 6 servings · Prep: 20 minutes · Cook: 120 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 lb Boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 2.5 lb Baby potatoes
  • 2 lb Brussels sprouts
  • 1 cup Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil, divided
  • 5 cloves Garlic, minced, divided
  • 1 whole Lemon, zest and juice
  • 1 tsp Dried rosemary
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Onion powder
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt, divided
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper, divided
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Preheat the smoker to 275°F. Pat chicken dry. Reserve half the salt and pepper for the vegetables. Rub chicken with 1 tablespoon olive oil, rosemary, thyme, garlic powder, onion powder, remaining salt and pepper, and zest of half the lemon.
  2. Smoke chicken until the thickest part of each breast reaches 165°F, approximately 1½–2 hours depending on thickness. Cook to temperature rather than time. Rest 5–10 minutes before slicing.
  3. While chicken smokes, heat oven to 425°F. Halve baby potatoes; trim and halve Brussels sprouts. Toss potatoes with 1 tablespoon olive oil and 2 minced garlic cloves. Toss sprouts with the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil and 2 minced garlic cloves. Divide reserved salt and pepper between the vegetables.
  4. Spread vegetables on separate sheet pans. Roast potatoes 30–40 minutes and sprouts 20–30 minutes, turning halfway, until browned and tender. Time the roasting to finish near the chicken.
  5. Stir Greek yogurt with the remaining garlic clove, remaining lemon zest, lemon juice and parsley. Add a little water to thin if desired.
  6. Divide sliced chicken, potatoes, sprouts and yogurt sauce into six servings. Refrigerate lunch portions promptly, keeping sauce separate. Reheat chicken to 165°F.
