Mediterranean Smoked Chicken Bowls with Farro and Tzatziki

Wednesday dinner. Smoked thighs, fiber-rich farro and chickpeas, fresh vegetables, feta and homemade tzatziki.

Yield: 6 servings · Prep: 25 minutes · Cook: 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2.75 lb Boneless skinless chicken thighs; raw; smoke Saturday and freeze for Wednesday
  • 1.5 cup Dry farro
  • 2 can Chickpeas; 15 oz each, drained and rinsed
  • 2 whole Cucumber; one for bowls, one grated for tzatziki
  • 1.5 pint Cherry tomatoes; halved
  • 1 whole Red onion; finely diced
  • 0.75 cup Crumbled feta
  • 1.5 cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 whole Lemon; juice divided
  • 0.25 cup Fresh dill; chopped
  • 3 clove Garlic; minced, divided
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1.5 tsp Kosher salt; or to taste
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper

Instructions

  1. On Saturday season chicken thighs with oregano, paprika, salt and pepper. Smoke at 275°F to 175–185°F internal. Chill promptly, freeze for Wednesday and thaw in refrigerator Tuesday.
  2. Cook farro according to package directions until tender, then drain.
  3. Grate one cucumber, squeeze out excess water and mix with yogurt, dill, half the minced garlic and juice of one lemon to make tzatziki.
  4. Dice remaining cucumber and combine with halved tomatoes, red onion, chickpeas, olive oil, remaining garlic and juice of one lemon.
  5. Reheat chicken until 165°F, then slice.
  6. Divide farro, chicken, chickpea-vegetable mixture and feta between six bowls. Serve tzatziki separately.
  7. Refrigerate two lunch portions promptly, keeping sauce and fresh vegetables separate where possible.
