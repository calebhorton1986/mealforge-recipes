Blueberry Cheesecake Protein Baked Oats

Monday–Thursday breakfast for two adults. High-protein baked oats with blueberries, chia and a creamy cheesecake-style topping.

Yield: 8 servings · Prep: 20 minutes · Cook: 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 cup Old-fashioned rolled oats; dry
  • 4 scoop Vanilla protein powder; approximately 120 g
  • 2 cup Low-fat cottage cheese; blend smooth
  • 2 cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt; divided: 1 cup batter, 1 cup topping
  • 2 whole Large eggs
  • 1 cup Liquid egg whites
  • 1.5 cup Milk, 2%
  • 2.5 cup Blueberries; fresh or frozen
  • 0.5 cup Chia seeds
  • 2 tsp Baking powder
  • 2 tsp Vanilla extract
  • 1 whole Lemon; zest and 1 tbsp juice
  • 0.25 cup Maple syrup
  • 4 oz Light cream cheese; softened, for topping
  • 1 portion Cooking spray; for baking dish

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F and lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, 1 cup Greek yogurt, eggs, egg whites, milk, vanilla, lemon zest and maple syrup until smooth.
  3. In a large bowl mix oats, protein powder, chia seeds and baking powder. Stir in blended wet ingredients, then fold in 2 cups blueberries.
  4. Spread batter in baking dish and scatter remaining 1/2 cup blueberries over top.
  5. Bake 35–45 minutes until center is set. Cool at least 15 minutes before cutting into eight squares.
  6. Beat remaining 1 cup Greek yogurt with softened cream cheese and lemon juice for cheesecake topping. Refrigerate separately.
  7. Cool casserole promptly; refrigerate portions for up to 3–4 days and freeze later portions if needed. Top after reheating. Reheat until hot.
