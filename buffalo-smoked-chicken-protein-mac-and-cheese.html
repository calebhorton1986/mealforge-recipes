Buffalo Smoked Chicken Protein Mac and Cheese

Monday dinner. Smoky chicken breast, high-protein pasta and broccoli in a creamy buffalo-cheddar sauce.

Yield: 6 servings · Prep: 20 minutes · Cook: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2.75 lb Boneless skinless chicken breast; raw; smoke Saturday
  • 16 oz High-protein pasta; dry elbows or rotini
  • 1.5 lb Broccoli florets; fresh or frozen
  • 1 cup Plain nonfat Greek yogurt; room temperature
  • 1.5 cup Reduced-fat shredded cheddar
  • 1 cup Milk, 2%
  • 0.5 cup Buffalo wing sauce; adjust to taste
  • 0.5 cup Chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tsp Garlic powder; divided
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Kosher salt; or to taste
  • 0.5 tsp Black pepper

Instructions

  1. On Saturday season chicken with 1 tsp garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Smoke at 275°F until thickest portion reaches 165°F. Cool promptly, refrigerate for Monday and dice before using.
  2. Cook pasta until just tender. Add broccoli during final 3–4 minutes if fresh, or cook separately if frozen. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain.
  3. In a large pot warm olive oil, milk, broth, remaining garlic powder and buffalo sauce over medium-low heat. Stir in cheddar until melted; avoid boiling.
  4. Remove from heat and whisk in Greek yogurt. Thin with pasta water as needed.
  5. Fold in pasta, broccoli and diced smoked chicken. Return to gentle heat and warm until chicken reaches 165°F. Adjust buffalo sauce to taste.
  6. Divide into six portions; cool and refrigerate two for Tuesday lunch.
